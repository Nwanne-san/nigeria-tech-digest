Companies wishing to issue dollar-backed stablecoins in Nigeria now face a substantial minimum paid-up capital requirement of ₦15 billion, along with the need for a Nigerian-incorporated entity and sufficient readily accessible foreign currency (starting at $10 million) to meet local redemption requests. The requirement for local incorporation and operational liquidity in Nigeria underscores the CBN's intent to exert greater control over foreign currency transactions and prevent capital flight.
