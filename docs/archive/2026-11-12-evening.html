FG Clears 21-Month Pension Backlog, Funds Workers’ Benefits Until December 2029

Source: Nairametrics

What happened The Federal Government has successfully cleared a 21-month backlog in accrued pension rights for enrolled federal workers, a significant achievement announced by the National Pension Commission (PenCom). Omolola Oloworaran, the Director-General of PenCom, stated at the 2026 Pension Correspondents Association of Nigeria Conference that the government has moved from being 21 months in arrears to maintaining a 41-month funding surplus. This means that accrued pension rights for federal employees who have not yet retired are now funded up to December 2029. Furthermore, delays in pension payments have been largely eliminated, with retirement benefits now processed within a maximum of 48 hours. The PenCom boss also highlighted the launch of the PenCare health insurance programme and a N52,500 minimum pension guarantee by President Bola Tinubu.

Context This is a profoundly positive development for the National Progress & Economy storyline, directly addressing a long-standing issue that has plagued public sector retirees and workers. The clearance of a 21-month backlog and the creation of a 41-month funding surplus represent a significant improvement in fiscal discipline and commitment to social welfare. Historically, pension arrears have been a source of immense hardship and distrust in government, contributing to the "painful first term" sentiment mentioned in other reports. This move provides "greater certainty over their earned benefits" and signals a more stable and reliable Contributory Pension Scheme, boosting confidence in government's ability to manage its obligations.
