This is particularly critical at a time when Nigeria is actively seeking foreign direct investment and capital deepening. The article suggests that instead of diverting these funds, the government should focus on addressing the systemic issues that lead to such a large volume of unclaimed dividends, such as improving the e-dividend registration process and simplifying probate requirements.

## Outlook & Speculation

*   **AI Model Democratization Continues:** Expect the trend of highly capable, yet cost-effective, open-weight models to accelerate. This will likely lead to a wider adoption of AI by smaller businesses and individual developers in Nigeria, fostering innovation in niche applications.

*   **AI Governance Gap Widens:** The rapid pace of AI development will continue to outstrip regulatory frameworks globally and in Nigeria. This will create ongoing challenges in areas like data privacy, intellectual property, and accountability for AI-driven decisions.

*   **Cybersecurity Threats Evolve with AI:** The use of AI for both offense and defense will intensify. We can anticipate more sophisticated AI-powered cyberattacks, but also more advanced AI-driven security solutions. Developers will need to be vigilant about AI-specific vulnerabilities.

*   **Sovereign Cloud Initiatives Drive Local Tech Ecosystems:** Nigeria's National Sovereign Cloud Initiative, alongside similar efforts in other nations, will likely spur significant investment and growth in local cloud infrastructure and AI talent. This could create new opportunities for Nigerian tech companies and developers.

*   **The "AI Slop" Problem Persists:** Despite user pushback, the proliferation of low-quality, AI-generated content is likely to continue, posing challenges for information integrity. This will increase the demand for tools and expertise in AI content verification and curation.

*   **Enterprise AI Adoption Becomes More Pragmatic:** Businesses will move beyond hype to focus on practical AI applications that demonstrate clear ROI. This will drive demand for AI engineers who can integrate AI solutions into existing workflows and solve specific business problems, rather than just building theoretical models.

*   **Geopolitical Factors Influence AI Hardware Access:** The global competition for AI hardware, coupled with geopolitical tensions, could impact the availability and cost of essential components for AI development, potentially influencing Nigeria's ability to scale its AI infrastructure.
