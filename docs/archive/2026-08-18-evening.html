Presidential Candidates Sign Peace Accord, Warn Against AI Misinformation

Source: BusinessDay, Vanguard, Punch

What happened: President Bola Tinubu, represented by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, George Akume, signed the 2027 election Peace Accord in Abuja on Tuesday. The explicit mention of "AI misinformation" by President Tinubu is a new and critical dimension, reflecting a growing global concern about the weaponization of generative AI in electoral processes. The emphasis on AI misinformation highlights a nascent but critical challenge for Nigeria's democracy. Independent Research Reveals Discrepancies in Reported AI Usage, Highlighting Sensitive Behaviors

Source: MIT Tech Review

What happened: A new research project, the AI Observatory, co-led by Anka Reuel of the Stanford Trustworthy AI Research (STAIR) Lab, has found significant discrepancies between how AI companies like Anthropic and OpenAI report AI usage and actual user behavior. The AI Observatory, a public platform that aggregated and analyzed 85,633 conversational turns from 24,521 conversations across seven datasets (2023-2025), found that nearly half (48%) of conversations filtered out by Anthropic's work-focused "Economic Index" involved sensitive topics. These included health and relationships (44.2% vs 31.2% in Anthropic's analysis), adult or illicit content (7.9% vs 2.1%), harassment and hate (27.5% vs 5.66%), and sexual content (16.7% vs 2.4%). The study also revealed differences across models: Grok and Gemini were used more for information retrieval (Grok for news/politics, with misinformation concentration), Anthropic for coding, Gemini for social/roleplay, and ChatGPT for homework. Conversations also grew longer and more elaborate over time, with increasing small talk suggesting AI companionship, while AI assistants' self-disclosure decreased.

Deep Lore / Context: This groundbreaking research by the AI Observatory directly challenges the narratives presented by major AI companies, significantly escalating our "AI Personality and User Interaction" and "AI Governance and Regulation Gap" storylines.
