1. INEC Targets Dec 15 For Publication Of Final Voters’ Register (Daily Trust)

What happened: The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) has set December 15, 2026, as the target date for the final publication of the voters’ register ahead of the 2027 general elections. Professor Joash Amupitan, Chairman of INEC, made this announcement at the South-South Zonal Interactive Meeting with INEC Staff in Port Harcourt, Rivers State. Amupitan addressed concerns regarding allegedly AI-generated photographs appearing on the Commission’s preliminary voters’ register, stating that these records are currently under examination for potential corrective action. He urged Nigerians to actively participate in the ongoing display, claims, and objections process to identify any irregularities. The INEC Chairman also disclosed plans to strengthen the Commission’s technological capacity, including the deployment of 57,000 newly procured Bimodal Voter Accreditation System (BVAS) devices and an audit of electronic result management systems to enhance transparency via the INEC Result Viewing (IReV) portal. The South-South geopolitical zone currently accounts for 15,823,550 registered voters, with Rivers State having 3,736,434.

The report details that the ₦95.5 billion valuation, when measured against the actual cash invested, represents a significant loss. Specifically, against the ₦107.95 billion invested by December 2025, it's a 12% loss, and against the ₦137.95 billion invested by September 2026, it's a 31% loss. The "premium" was calculated against a heavily written-down paper value of ₦45.39 billion, which itself was a result of ₦63.06 billion in impairment charges by December 2025. The article also highlighted a puzzling trend within MoMo PSB: customer deposits surged to ₦43.82 billion by September 30, 2026, from ₦9.84 billion in December 2025, yet active wallets simultaneously fell by 36% to 3.2 million. This suggests that the deposit growth might be driven by large corporate or high-net-worth placements rather than widespread adoption by everyday mobile money users.

Deep Lore / Context: This detailed financial scrutiny of MTN Nigeria's fintech transaction provides a crucial, nuanced perspective on the health and direction of Nigeria's burgeoning fintech sector, directly impacting the "Fintech Diversification and Business Infrastructure" and "CBN Policy and Economic Management" storylines.
