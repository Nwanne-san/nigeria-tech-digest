### FG Eyes Unclaimed Dividends, Dormant Funds for NELFUND
**Source:** Daily Trust

**What happened:** President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has directed the transfer of legally cleared funds recovered by the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) to the Nigerian Education Loan Fund (NELFUND). In a further move to bolster the fund's sustainability, the President also directed that unclaimed dividends from capital market trust funds and funds held in the dormant account trust fund be considered for transfer to NELFUND, provided these actions comply with existing laws. Minister of Education, Dr. Maruf Tunji Alausa, disclosed this on Monday, emphasizing that these measures are designed to ensure NELFUND's financial buoyancy "into the near future, into the distant future and sustainably to eternity." **Deep Lore / Context:** This initiative marks a significant escalation in the government's strategy to secure sustainable funding for the student loan scheme, a cornerstone promise of the current administration aimed at expanding access to higher education.
