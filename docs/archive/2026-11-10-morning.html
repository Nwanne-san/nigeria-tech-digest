Source: Sahara Reporters What happened: The Federal High Court in Abuja has issued a subpoena compelling the Chairman of the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), Professor Joash Ojo Amupitan, to appear in court and present electoral documents related to President Bola Tinubu's governorship candidacy in 1999 and his presidential candidacy in 2023. This subpoena, issued in Charge No. FHC/CR/484/2025, is connected to the ongoing criminal trial of human rights activist and African Action Congress (AAC) presidential candidate, Omoyele Sowore, who is facing charges for describing President Tinubu as a criminal. However, INEC Chairman Amupitan has stated that as of Monday, November 9, 2026, he had not yet received a Certified True Copy of the court order, emphasizing that the commission does not act based on social media reports.

Source: The New Stack What happened: Google's announcement of its eighth-generation TPUs included the introduction of TPU Sync, an open-source system designed to drastically reduce AI inference latency.
