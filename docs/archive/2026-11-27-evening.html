Source: The New Stack What happened: Guillermo Rauch, CEO of Vercel, a platform for frontend developers, posits that the next significant gains in AI performance will not primarily come from developing smarter models, but rather from improvements in the "harness" – the underlying infrastructure that executes AI agents. These improvements, particularly noticeable in parallel tasks, were achieved without changing models or modifying prompts.
