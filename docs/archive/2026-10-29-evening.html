INEC Clears Pantami, Gwarzo for 2027 Elections, Rejects Adebutu

Source: The Sun, Vanguard, Daily Trust

What happened: The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) concluded a marathon meeting on Thursday, granting provisional clearance to former Minister of Communications and Digital Economy, Isa Ali Pantami, for the Gombe State governorship race, and Abdulsalam Gwarzo, the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) governorship candidate in Kano State, to contest the 2027 general elections. This clearance, however, remains subject to the outcomes of pending court cases. Conversely, the commission rejected the petition from Ladi Adebutu, the Ogun State Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) governorship candidate, who had sought a review of his exclusion. INEC received a total of 69 petitions from political parties concerning omitted candidates for governorship and State Houses of Assembly elections, with approximately 36 deemed successful.

Deep Lore / Context: This development marks a significant turn in the Political Integrity and Electoral Processes (Nigeria) storyline, which we've been tracking as escalating. The issue of candidate eligibility has been a recurring flashpoint, particularly since the Supreme Court's September 24, 2026, judgment restored Sections 77(5), (6), (7), and 84(2) of the Electoral Act 2026. These provisions mandate parties to submit their membership registers to INEC within stipulated times, a requirement that led to the initial omission of several prominent names, including Pantami's, from the provisional lists. The commission had attributed these omissions to alleged non-compliance with these provisions. The provisional clearance for Pantami and Gwarzo, despite ongoing litigation, indicates INEC's attempt to balance legal compliance with administrative flexibility, though the final word will rest with the judiciary. This situation highlights the complex interplay between electoral law, party politics, and judicial intervention that continues to shape our democratic process.
