**Headline:** 2027: INEC Orders RECs To Map Election Violence Flashpoints (Daily Trust)

**What happened:** The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) Chairman, Prof. Joash Amupitan, has directed Resident Electoral Commissioners (RECs) to identify potential election violence hotspots across all wards and local government areas ahead of the 2027 general elections. **Deep Lore / Context:** This directive from INEC is a direct response to the *escalating* "Political Integrity and Electoral Processes (Nigeria)" storyline, which has been tracking early campaign activities marked by reported political violence and strategic maneuvering. ### 1. Local Hardware Becomes Bottleneck for AI Agent Orchestration

**Headline:** A developer ran 50 agents at once. Then his Mac became the bottleneck. (The New Stack)

**What happened:** A developer named Pranit shared on X (formerly Twitter) how attempting to run more than 50 concurrent AI coding agent sessions exposed a critical bottleneck in his development workflow: his local Mac hardware. Vercel CTO Malte Ubl confirmed encountering similar limitations, stating, "No single computer is fast enough to keep up with dozens of agents working on parallel." Ubl's proposed solution involves keeping the AI agents running locally for orchestration but offloading computationally intensive tasks like builds and tests to remote sandboxes. This approach allows developers to maintain their existing tools and workflows while scaling workloads independently of local hardware constraints. The article highlights that even when model inference occurs in the cloud, compilation, dependency management, and test execution initiated by coding agents can heavily tax local CPU and memory. Vercel offers a plugin to facilitate this remote execution, which begins from a snapshot with dependencies already installed and copies current source code to the sandbox, allowing agents to validate changes before committing without local resource competition.

**Deep Lore / Context:** This story offers a crucial insight into the practical challenges emerging from the *escalating* "AI Model Competition and Enterprise Adoption" storyline, particularly the rapid innovation in agentic systems. It also indirectly touches upon the *developing* "Nigeria's Digital Skills and AI Workforce Development" storyline, as infrastructure gaps, even at the individual developer level, can hinder advanced AI engineering work.

The solution proposed by Vercel CTO Malte Ubl – separating agent orchestration from code execution by offloading heavy tasks to remote sandboxes – signals a significant architectural shift in software development workflows.
