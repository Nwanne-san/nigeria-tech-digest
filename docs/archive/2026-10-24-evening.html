Tinubu-Atiku Clash Ignites 2027 Campaign with Blame Game Over Hardship

Source: Daily Trust, Vanguard, Channels TV, Punch

What happened: President Bola Tinubu, at the All Progressives Congress (APC) 2027 presidential campaign flag-off in Kaduna, asserted that opposition politicians were responsible for the prevailing hunger and poverty in Nigeria, stating, "They created the hunger, they created the poverty. Pennsylvania Will Require Labels on AI-Generated Ads

Source: The Verge

What happened: Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro signed the Artificial Intelligence Deceptive Advertising Act into law, making it one of the first states to require disclosures on AI-generated advertisements. The law mandates that any ad—whether still image, video, audio, or text—that has been "significantly modified or generated by artificial intelligence" must include a label in the same format as the ad itself. This applies regardless of where the ad was created, meaning disclosures could appear in commercials airing in other states if they are also expected to air in Pennsylvania. However, the law contains several loopholes, including subjective definitions of "significantly modified" and "evidently fictional," and carve-outs for "parody, satire, commentary or criticism."

Context: This legislative action is a notable development in the "AI Governance and Regulation Gap" storyline, demonstrating an early attempt by a state government to address the challenges of AI-generated content, particularly disinformation and consumer deception. While the law's loopholes suggest initial difficulties in comprehensive regulation, its existence signals a growing global trend towards requiring transparency for AI-generated media.
