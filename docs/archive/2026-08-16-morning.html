Deep Lore / Context: This is a significant escalation in the political discourse surrounding the current administration's economic management and accountability, directly following previous concerns about Nigeria's economy showing mixed signals and the effectiveness of public spending. Expect continued government focus on rebuilding critical infrastructure in previously conflict-affected areas.

This indicates a likely continued flow of foreign capital, driving innovation and expansion in the sector, but also raising questions about local ownership and control.
