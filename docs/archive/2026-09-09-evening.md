**Headline:** Tinubu’s FAAC boom is a scam — Atiku (Daily Trust)
**What happened:** Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has strongly criticized the Federal Government's celebration of increased Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) distributions, calling it a "monetary illusion" and a "scam." The core of Atiku's argument revolves around the concept of "money illusion," where nominal increases in currency figures mask a decline in real purchasing power due to high inflation and currency depreciation. This critique resonates deeply with ordinary Nigerians who experience the daily struggle of rising costs for essential goods and services, despite official reports of increased government revenue. The debate highlights a fundamental disconnect between macroeconomic indicators presented by the government and the microeconomic realities faced by households and businesses.

**Context:** This is a significant technical improvement for developers using Google Cloud's Spanner, enhancing its flexibility and scalability for complex transactional workloads.
