Source: Sahara Reporters

What happened The Tanimu Turaki-led faction of the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) has accused the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) of actively working to hijack and undermine the party, with the alleged goal of replacing its presidential candidate, former President Goodluck Jonathan, ahead of the 2027 general elections. Deep Lore / Context This is a significant escalation in the ongoing internal turmoil within the PDP, a major opposition party, and raises serious questions about the neutrality and integrity of INEC as we approach the 2027 general elections. The Supreme Court judgment mentioned in the article likely refers to a past ruling that invalidated a previous leadership structure, creating the vacuum that the Turaki-led interim committee claims to fill. The accusation that INEC is deliberately refusing to recognize a faction and withholding access codes for electronic submissions, while allegedly denying congresses it monitored, follows a pattern of mistrust and allegations of bias that have historically surrounded Nigeria's electoral body.
